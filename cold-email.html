The moment you lead with your hourly rate, you make it easier for people to compare you to everyone else who sells time. That does not mean your rate is wrong. It means the frame is wrong. Not your services. Not your modalities. The actual outcomes. The marriage that survived. The case that settled. The body that healed. The business that scaled. The person who got more confident.
